The paper industry has been credited with saving the art world from becoming saturated with watermarks by using less paper and by eliminating waste. Watermarking has also been credited with saving the art world from becoming saturated with chemicals.

The art of watermarking can be traced to an ingenious process called “melt-reading” (translating watermarks onto paper by adding a color to a piece of paper) in the 16th century. According to the historian Leonardo da Vinci, the process could have been called “melt-reading” (translating a piece of paper onto a piece of paper and then adding a color to it).
